Unmodified FontForge SFD sources and TTF files are included here.
`bitmap.json` is the monochrome 23 x 29 derivative produced by
`scripts/build-aircraft-font.py`; square placeholders are generated by that script. At runtime, src/mcdu/display.rs
creates three bold capital derivatives (D/O/M) in unused lowercase slots for
the red DOOM menu entry. The normal uppercase and small font banks are preserved.

Page labels, colors and placement follow the MCDU legacy page templates from
the same revision, adapted to a standalone Rust state machine. The FlyByWire
authors retain rights to that reference material. No flight simulator code,
trademarks, logos or aircraft models are included. This project is unaffiliated
with FlyByWire Simulations, Airbus, Honeywell and WINCTRL.
